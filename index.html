About Me

My name is Ojandu Ronny. I am a 19-year-old Ugandan student at Muni University, pursuing a Bachelor of Information Technology. I am developing my web development skills with guidance from my lecturer and mentor, Mr. Andogah Geoffrey. This website is part of my practical learning in writing clear, semantic HTML, styling pages with CSS, and adding interactivity with JavaScript. I enjoy learning new things and exploring web development.

My Background

I began learning web development through an introduction to HTML at Ocer Campion Jesuit College in Gulu, where I completed my Advanced Level studies. I completed my Ordinary Level education at St. Andrew's College in Moyo. Creating this profile is one of my first steps towards becoming more confident in web development. With practice and guidance, I hope to continue improving my skills and produce useful digital projects.
